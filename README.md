# 🔴🟡🟢🟣 Social Games (Party Games Hub)

![Kotlin](https://img.shields.io/badge/Kotlin-2.0-blue)
![Jetpack Compose](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jetpack%20Compose-Material%203-purple)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-orange)

**Social Games** is an all-in-one party game hub for Android built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3. Play multiple engaging party games with friends on a single phone!

---

## 🎮 Included Games & Modes

1. **🎭 Imposter Who? (İmposter Kimdir?)**:
   * Find the betrayer among civilians!
   * Modes: Classic, Undercover, Spy, Mr. White, Troll, Duo.
   * 3 Difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, 9th-Grade Hard).
   * Inverse-frequency weighted imposter selection algorithm.
   * Among Us style space ejection animation screen.

2. **✍️ Who, With Whom? (Kim, Kimlə, Harada, Ne vaxt, Ne edir, Kim gördü?)**:
   * Dynamic party story creation for 3 to 6 players!
   * **3 Players**: Kim?, Kimlə?, Ne edir?
   * **4 Players**: Kim?, Kimlə?, Harada?, Ne edir?
   * **5 Players**: Kim?, Kimlə?, Harada?, Ne edir?, Kim gördü?
   * **6 Players**: Kim?, Kimlə?, Harada?, Ne vaxt?, Ne edir?, Kim gördü?

3. **🍾 Truth or Dare (Düzlük yoxsa Cəsarət?)**:
   * **9th Grade (Teen/School)**: Friendly school, crush, exam and fun dares.
   * **18+ (Adult)**: Spicy party truths and bold dares.

4. **🥔 Word Explaining & Hot Potato (Söz İzahı Və Qaynar Kartof)**:
   * Rapid-fire word explanation before the hot potato explodes!

5. **🎭 Who Am I? (Mən Kiməm?)**:
   * Random target character assignment (e.g. Player A assigns secret persona for Player C, B for D, etc.).
   * Friends give **Hot / Cold / Extremely Close / Correct** feedback.
   * Persistence lock: Remains active until the player finds their identity!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Language**: Kotlin 2.0
* **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose with Material 3 Design
* **Architecture**: MVVM with Android ViewModel
* **Storage**: Android SharedPreferences (Persistent State & Settings)
* **Minimum SDK**: API 24 (Android 7.0 Nougat)
* **Target SDK**: API 37
